Overload Dice Privacy Policy

gagadodo · kmymgd@gmail.com · 2026-10-12

Summary

Overload Dice is an offline game that runs only on your device without the internet. It has no accounts, ads, payments or analytics, and the game sends nothing to the developer automatically. Information that can be tied to a person only reaches the developer when you send a feedback email yourself (explained below).

Information stored on this device

Stored: the run in progress, settings (sound, music, volume, effects intensity, vibration), unlocked items, best records and recent play history (up to 50 runs: date, mode, seed, result, score, loadout and abilities used).

So that the previous state survives an interrupted save, a recovery copy of the same data is kept in the app's private storage.

This information lives only in the app's private storage and other apps can't read it. The app explicitly opts out of Android Auto Backup (cloud backup) and device-to-device transfer. So when you change devices these records are not moved and you start fresh on the new device. Separate transfer tools from the manufacturer may behave differently depending on the manufacturer.

Deletion and retention

You can erase progress, records and settings at once under Settings > Data management, and the recovery copy is erased too.

Uninstalling the app removes all stored information. It is kept until you delete it (or uninstall the app).

External actions that happen only when you tap

Send feedback: the mail app's compose screen opens. The body is pre-filled with the app version, Android version and device model, and you can read, edit or delete it before sending. Progress, seeds, save files, device identifiers and logs are never attached automatically. The email is only sent when you tap Send in your mail app.

When you send an email, your email address and its contents (including the pre-filled app and device info) reach the developer (kmymgd@gmail.com). They're used only to answer feedback and fix problems and aren't given to anyone else. No automatic deletion period is set; to have it deleted, ask at the same address. The email itself also remains in your own email service.

Share: the result text or install file (APK) you chose is handed to Android's share sheet. You pick the receiving app and person, and how the chosen app handles the content follows that app's own policy.

Rate: once this game is listed on Google Play, the Play page opens. Before it's listed, nothing opens and only a notice is shown.

Permissions

Only the vibration permission is used (a short vibration if you turn it on in settings). No internet, storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions are used.

Children and third parties

The game doesn't collect anything automatically, so there's no information handled differently by age. The contents of a feedback email you send aren't used for any purpose other than handling it, and aren't given to third parties. What you send via Share goes to the app you choose.

Contact and changes

Contact: kmymgd@gmail.com (developer gagadodo, https://wekinu.github.io/).

If a feature change alters what information is handled, we'll update this document and the app version together and show the changed date above.
