Send and receive with QRTransfer.

  1. Install QRTransfer on both phones.
  2. On the sending phone, open Send → Photo and choose an image.
  3. Review the size and transfer guidance. Resize a larger photo when useful; the limit is 3MB.
  4. On the other phone, open Receive and point the camera at the sender's QR display.
  5. Hold both phones steady. When the receiver says Received, stop sending and save the photo on the receiver.

The picture shown is a small synthetic demo image. Its estimated time is not a promise for a typical photo.

Does QR photo sharing need the internet?

The visual QR transfer itself does not. Ads, purchases and downloading the app do use the internet.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size, camera, screen brightness and movement. Large photos may take several minutes. Resize first when the full image resolution is unnecessary.

Can any QR scanner receive the photo?

No. Photos and continuous QR need QRTransfer on both phones. Short plain text in a single ordinary QR is a different case.

Are all file transfers Bluetooth-free?

No. The separate fast file transfer feature for compatible Samsung Galaxy phones supports files up to 1MB and requires Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. The photo/text visual QR method on this page is the Bluetooth-free method.

Is visual QR sharing encrypted?

No. Anyone who can see the screen may be able to read the content. Choose what you share and where you share it.
